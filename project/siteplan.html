Ghana Chamber of Commerce - Site Plan

Site Name

Ghana Chamber of Commerce

This name clearly represents the official website of a chamber promoting business and trade development in Ghana. It’s a professional and recognizable name suitable for the organization’s objectives.

Optional domain: ghanachamber.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide useful resources and information for businesses, investors, and members of the Ghana Chamber of Commerce. The site will offer business news, upcoming events, membership information, and a directory of affiliated companies.

Scenarios

  • How can I become a registered member of the Ghana Chamber of Commerce?
  • Where can I find upcoming business networking events or trade fairs in Ghana?

Color Schema

The site will use the following color scheme:

  • Primary Color: Navy Blue (#004080) – used for headings and navigation bar.
  • Accent Color: Gold/Orange (#f4a300) – used for buttons, highlights, and calls to action.

Typography

  • Headings: Roboto Slab – A strong, professional serif font for headers.
  • Body Text: Open Sans – A clean, modern sans-serif for readability in paragraphs and general content.
